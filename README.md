### About me

I'm interested in the intersection of **robotics, control theory, and machine learning**, especially how ideas become reliable behavior on physical robots.

My work and learning interests include **legged locomotion**, **robotic manipulation**, **model-based control**, and **robot learning**. I'm also exploring how **multimodal perception**, **spatial intelligence**, **world models**, and **robot agents** fit into the bigger picture.

I value clear explanations, reproducible experiments, and learning by building.

### What I'm exploring

| Area | Topics |
| --- | --- |
| **Robotics foundations** | Kinematics, dynamics, contact, sensing, embedded systems |
| **Planning & control** | Optimal and nonlinear control, motion planning, whole-body control |
| **Robot learning** | Reinforcement learning, imitation learning, sim-to-real |
| **Embodied intelligence** | Multimodal perception, spatial reasoning, VLA, world models, agents |

### Wichun.lab 🌱

**Wichun.lab** is my growing open-learning initiative: a place to connect the dots from a robot's physical body to its ability to perceive, plan, act, and learn.

The goal is to gradually share:

- **Learning notes** — concepts, mathematics, and clear technical explanations
- **Paper readings** — research ideas, comparisons, and open questions
- **Hands-on projects** — code, simulations, and reproducible experiments
- **A living roadmap** — an evolving map of the robotics and embodied AI landscape

The first [Wichun.lab knowledge map and project overview](https://github.com/wenyu-robot/wichun-lab) are now available in English and Chinese. Follow the repository for upcoming learning notes, paper readings, and reproducible experiments.

### How I learn

**Explore → Understand → Build → Share**

I prefer understanding *why* a method works, checking it against experiments, and making the result useful to others.
